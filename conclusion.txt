Conclusion - The progression of a psy-op

I have only been the victim of one psy-op so I can't speak to all of them but this was the progression of events for me:

- They start slow, and quiet.  They don't want you to know so it'll be subtle negging by strangers, gaslighting and manipulation by people posing as friends. Perhaps an attempt to set you up with social media.
- establish surveilance.  Hack your phone, bug your house, get your neighbors involved so they can monitor you.
- try one of the simpler tricks, incitement to violence in my case
- if this fails and there is evidence they will try to get you to kill yourself.
- go quiet again, maintain subtle manipulation, try to implicate you in crimes
- attempt to isolate you, go loud and try to get you to kill yourself
- get sophisticated and try to get you to kill yourself

At every stage, your best bet is to collect evidence and report to the police or relevant authority.  Keep written records.  Research your rights and exercise them calmly but firmly.

At every stage, they will try to get leverage over you and isolate you further from friends, family and institutions.

They do not care about public safety whatsoever, and would be quite happy for you to flip out and harm an innocent member of the public.
