# Implied Consequences

**Effects:** Uncertainty, apprehension, rumination, self-censorship.

**Possible objectives:** Create apprehension, discourage an action, provoke speculation about unspecified consequences.

## The technique

A statement such as "Keep digging!" can imply that someone should carry on doing what they are doing and will get themselves into even more trouble as a result.

Neither part of the message has to be explained. The speaker does not say exactly what action they mean, what trouble is supposed to follow, or how it will happen.

Other examples include:

- "Carry on. See where that gets you."
- "You're only making things worse for yourself."
- "You'll find out."
- "Keep going and you'll see."

The words can be presented as a taunt, a warning or a knowing remark. The important feature is that the action and the consequences are left for the listener to determine.

## How it works

The statement gives the impression that the speaker knows where things are heading, but withholds the information that would make the implication clear.

The listener is left to supply the missing details:

- What am I supposedly doing?
- What trouble am I getting into?
- Who or what is going to cause it?
- How serious are the consequences?

Because none of this is specified, the listener may start searching their own behaviour for an explanation and imagining possible outcomes. The ambiguity can make an ordinary action feel risky, or make someone hesitate over something they would otherwise have continued doing.

This is different from a direct threat, which identifies at least some action or consequence. It is also different from a statement that someone has crossed a line. Here, the implication is that continuing with an unspecified action will lead to unspecified trouble.

## The effect

The listener may become preoccupied with working out what the speaker meant. They may stop an ordinary activity, change their plans or become reluctant to pursue something legitimate because they are trying to avoid consequences that have never actually been identified.

The phrase itself does not establish that the speaker knows anything about the listener's actions or has any ability to bring about the suggested consequences.

You do not have to solve the riddle. If there is a specific, credible threat, deal with it on the facts and take appropriate steps to stay safe. Otherwise, an ambiguous remark does not need to change what you are doing.

## Response

**Don't engage at all. Record if convenient, then get on with your day.**

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, get to safety and call **999**. Seeking emergency help is not engaging with a provocation.

Read [the Golden Rule chapter](https://mikemayne.github.io/surviving-a-psyop/#read/record_ignore_report) for the standard response.
