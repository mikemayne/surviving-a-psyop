    A good place to start

    Whatever your circumstances, you can start with the four rules, and Record, Ignore, Report. You do not need to work out anyone else's intentions before choosing a calm, safe response.

    If you are in immediate danger in the UK, get to safety and call 999. For a concern that is not an emergency, consider talking with a trusted person, your GP or an appropriate professional.
