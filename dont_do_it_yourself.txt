# Don't Do It Yourself

**Effects:** Increase distress, Increase fear, Increase impulsivity.

**Possible objectives:** Increase the effectiveness of other techniques, Induce self-harm.

One possible purpose of sustained coercive pressure is to push someone into an action that the person exerting the pressure cannot or will not take directly.

This can include provoking aggression, self-harm or an impulsive decision that damages the person's life.

The manipulative element is the attempt to make a pressured decision appear entirely voluntary. Attention then shifts from the pressure itself to the action taken in response.

## Response

**Don't engage at all. Record if convenient, then get on with your day.**

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, get to safety and call **999**. Seeking emergency help is not engaging with a provocation.

Read [the Golden Rule chapter](https://mikemayne.github.io/surviving-a-psyop/#read/never_engage).

**If you don't respond, they have nothing to work with.**

**If you don't react, you don't give them anything to work with.**
