# Manufactured Urgency

**Effects:** Increase impulsivity, Increase pressure, Reduce time for reflection.

**Possible objectives:** Influence an immediate decision, Discourage verification, Provoke a reaction.

## The principle

Create the impression that someone must act immediately, even when there is no genuine need for haste.

The urgency becomes the reason to act. Instead of considering whether a request makes sense, the person is encouraged to worry about what will happen if they delay.

## How it works

Imagine someone says: "Decide RIGHT NOW or you'll lose your chance."

The supposed opportunity, deadline or consequence may never be explained. The listener is pushed to decide before checking the facts, weighing alternatives or seeking independent advice.

The same structure can be used with threats, offers, accusations or demands:

- "This is your last chance."
- "There's no time to check."
- "If you don't do something now, it'll be too late."
- "You have to make your mind up immediately."

**The mechanism is artificial time pressure: manufacture a deadline, discourage deliberation, and increase the chance of a rushed response.**

This is a meta-pattern because the specific action being demanded is interchangeable. It can overlap with The Imaginary Line, Implied Consequences, False Endings, Legal Intimidation and attempts to provoke impulsive behaviour.

A real emergency or a legitimate deadline is different. The pattern concerns time pressure that is invented, exaggerated or used to prevent reasonable consideration.

## Response

**Don't engage at all. Record if convenient, then get on with your day.**

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, get to safety and call **999**.
