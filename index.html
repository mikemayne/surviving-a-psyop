Interpreting your experiences

Recognising a technique described in this book does not mean it is being used against you. Look for evidence, avoid jumping to conclusions, and seek independent advice when unsure. Keeping a factual record of your experiences and discussing it with a medical professional is a sensible first step.

The aim of this book is to help you respond constructively, in such a way that will be helpful whether someone is targeting you or not.

About this material

A Note on Personal Experience and Professional Advice

This website describes events that I have personally experienced and the coping strategies I developed or adopted in response. I am sharing these experiences in the hope that others facing similar situations may find something useful in them.

I have no formal training or professional qualifications in mental health, medicine or law. The advice offered here is based entirely on my personal experience and should not be regarded as a substitute for professional medical, psychological or legal advice.

Some sections of this website discuss possible technical explanations for the experiences I describe. While I consider these explanations plausible, I do not claim to have independent proof that the techniques or technologies described exist or were used in the ways suggested, beyond my own experiences. These explanations should be understood as hypotheses rather than established facts, and I welcome alternative explanations and technical criticism.

I do not know what long-term negative consequences, if any, might arise from adopting the strategies described here. I cannot guarantee that they are safe, effective or appropriate for everyone. I can only say that I found them helpful, and that, in my own circumstances, I considered them preferable to the alternatives available to me.

I actively welcome independent scrutiny, constructive criticism and correction from anyone, but particularly from qualified mental health professionals, medical practitioners, psychologists, technical specialists and legal experts. If any of the strategies described here are potentially harmful, misleading or inconsistent with established professional guidance, I would very much like to know.

My objective is not to claim expertise or present my experiences as universal truths. It is to share what I have learned, encourage informed discussion, and make this material as useful, responsible and safe as possible for others.

What to do if you can't ignore

What's going to happen if you do nothing at all? If the answer is something dangerous, call the police. Otherwise read the four rules on the homepage. If you still can't ignore them try this:

Discuss whats been going on the day before with ChatGPT. Always leave 24 hours before you discuss anything.

Never check their statements in real time, because this gives them control over you.

If ChatGPT advises you to respond directly to provocative remarks, don't engage. Record, Ignore, Report. If you still want to discuss what happened, do so the next day.
