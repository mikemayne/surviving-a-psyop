# The Time Gap

Don't respond to provocations in real time. Don't respond after a predictable amount of time either.

If something concerns you, write it down, put it aside, and forget about it for now. Come back to it when it's convenient for you, if you still think it needs attention.

**Don't use the Analyzer, ChatGPT, or Google in real time in response to an abuser's actions.** Doing so allows someone trying to manipulate you to influence what you search for, what you type, and what you spend your time thinking about.

Responding immediately gives them instant feedback. Responding after a predictable delay can allow them to anticipate your behaviour.

**Following a predictable response pattern gives them a form of control over you. Deny them that control.**

You decide what deserves your attention, whether to respond, and when.

The only reason to act immediately is safety. If you feel threatened or in danger, contact the police. Call 999 if there is immediate danger.
