Social media

From How to Survive a Psyop — start with the four rules.

I don't use social media so I don't have much advice but I would advise you to only connect with people you are absolutely sure you know in person and either disconnect or ignore anyone that behaves strangely. In general, don't say anything online in private you wouldn't want to be shared with someone else.
