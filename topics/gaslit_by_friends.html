Recognising manipulation

Gaslighting by friends

From How to Survive a Psyop — start with the four rules.

Discusses friendship patterns that may undermine confidence, trust and personal boundaries.

Some signs you are being gaslit by a friend include:

- very different behaviour when you are in private and in public including: - encouraging a dark or controversial sense of humour in private and then behaving like its shocking in public - lauging hilariously at statements or jokes that you make that are not funny in private but reacting negatively to similar humour in public - consistently interrupting you when you are talking in public - disparaging remarks behind your back, but supportive remarks to your face - accusing you of not listening to them when you are very clearly listening to them - consistently encouraging self-destructive or anti-social behaviour, even if you express an intention to improve - encouraging you to share details of your life with them and then sharing them with people you obviously would not want to know, often in front of you - separating you from your friends or family - making you doubt your recollection of events by presenting an alternative reality you know to be untrue - encouraging you to commit illegal acts - deliberately provoking or negging you in public and then theatrically overreacting like you are being unreasonable - Consistently denying or minimising the impact of their behaviour while holding you responsible for your reactions.

If you recognise repeated occurances of one or more of these signs as a pattern of behaviour my recommendation is to store all communication from them and consider disengaging completely
