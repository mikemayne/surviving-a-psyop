Choose Your Death — Mock Executions and Coerced Choice

**Technique:** Choose your death is a form of simulated or mock execution in which an individual is led to believe that they are about to be killed, and that their own actions or decisions will determine how this happens. The threatened death may be presented as particularly brutal, humiliating or sexual in nature. Ordinary actions, arbitrary choices or responses to questions can be assigned supposed consequences, creating the impression that the individual is actively choosing the manner of their own death.

**Psychological objective:** The purpose is to induce extreme fear, helplessness and psychological distress while making the individual feel personally responsible for what is about to happen. By presenting an apparently unavoidable outcome as a choice between different forms of violence, the abuser attempts to deprive the individual of any sense of safety or control. Repetition can compound the effect, encouraging anticipatory fear and making even trivial decisions feel potentially catastrophic.

**Psychological mechanism:** The technique combines *death threats*, *coercive control*, *false dilemmas*, *anticipatory anxiety* and *traumatic conditioning*. The apparent choice is an illusion: the individual is offered alternatives within a situation they never consented to and should never have been placed in. The use of sexualised or degrading threats adds humiliation and vulnerability to the fear of death. Repeated exposure to apparent imminent danger can also produce hypervigilance and conditioned fear responses, even after the immediate threat has passed.

**An important feature is the deliberate transfer of responsibility from the abuser to the individual.** The person making the threats attempts to portray the threatened violence as the consequence of the individual's choices rather than their own deliberate actions. No choice made under such coercion constitutes meaningful consent or makes the threatened individual responsible for the abuse. Mock executions are a particularly severe form of psychological intimidation and, in some circumstances, constitute psychological torture.

Your options will be things that you inevitably have to do at some point, use the toilet, eat, everyday tasks. The fact that you eventually have to choose one of them will be presented as evidence of guilt.
