Don't chase

From How to Survive a Psyop — start with the four rules.

Don't chase them. Trying to catch them is counter-productive. Especially avoid doing anything unethical or illegal in your attempts to catch them, this will be used against you.

You cannot assume who they are, so you have to assume they have the surveillance advantage and know everything you are doing. They will use this to try to manipulate you, for example by staging conversations for you to overhear (see that section for more details).

They want you to engage with them. Ignoring them means they either have to go away, or behave increasingly recklessly to try to get your attention. In this case they are generating more evidence. Ignoring them can be difficult, so use the advice on the first page to avoid engaging with manipulative tactics.

They will misrepresent who they are at every step of the way, so if they tell you something write it down if you want to, but don't pay any attention to it. They could be one person or a national security agency, or anything inbetween.
