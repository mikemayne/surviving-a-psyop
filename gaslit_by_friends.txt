Some signs you are being gaslit by a friend include:

**Effects:** Increase distress, Increase confusion, Undermine confidence.

**Possible objectives:** Increase the effectiveness of other techniques, Induce compliance.

- very different behaviour when you are in private and in public including:
	- encouraging a dark or controversial sense of humour in private and then behaving like its shocking in public
	- lauging hilariously at statements or jokes that you make that are not funny in private but reacting negatively to similar humour in public
	- consistently interrupting you when you are talking in public
	- disparaging remarks behind your back, but supportive remarks to your face
- accusing you of not listening to them when you are very clearly listening to them
- consistently encouraging self-destructive or anti-social behaviour, even if you express an intention to improve
- encouraging you to share details of your life with them and then sharing them with people you obviously would not want to know, often in front of you
- separating you from your friends or family
- making you doubt your recollection of events by presenting an alternative reality you know to be untrue
- encouraging you to commit illegal acts
- deliberately provoking or negging you in public and then theatrically overreacting like you are being unreasonable
- Consistently denying or minimising the impact of their behaviour while holding you responsible for your reactions.

If you recognise repeated occurances of one or more of these signs as a pattern of behaviour my recommendation is to store all communication from them and consider disengaging completely

## Response

**Don't engage at all. Record if convenient, then get on with your day.**

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, get to safety and call **999**. Seeking emergency help is not engaging with a provocation.

Read [the Golden Rule chapter](https://mikemayne.github.io/surviving-a-psyop/#read/never_engage).

**If you don't respond, they have nothing to work with.**

**If you don't react, you don't give them anything to work with.**
