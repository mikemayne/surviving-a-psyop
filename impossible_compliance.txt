### **Impossible Compliance — Arbitrary Rules and Moving Goalposts**

**Technique:** Impossible compliance involves imposing arbitrary, contradictory or constantly changing expectations on an individual, ensuring that no amount of reasonable cooperation can satisfy the person making the demands. Even when the individual attempts to behave reasonably, accommodates legitimate requests and refuses only unreasonable or illegal demands, some trivial action can be identified as evidence of non-compliance. Ordinary activities, such as touching something red or eating an apple, can be retrospectively presented as violations of rules the individual never meaningfully agreed to.

**Psychological objective:** The purpose is to create a situation in which the individual cannot succeed, regardless of how reasonably they behave. By continually finding fault, the abuser attempts to generate frustration, helplessness, guilt and self-doubt. The individual may eventually become preoccupied with anticipating arbitrary objections rather than making independent decisions. It also allows the abuser to portray themselves as reasonable and cooperative while continually blaming the individual for the failure of compliance.

**Psychological mechanism:** The technique combines *moving the goalposts*, *double binds*, *arbitrary reinforcement* and elements of *learned helplessness*. When the standards for acceptable behaviour are unpredictable or impossible to satisfy, the individual may begin to perceive their actions as having little influence over the outcome. Trivial accusations also redirect attention away from whether the original demands were legitimate and towards defending otherwise harmless behaviour.

**An important feature is that compliance itself becomes the trap.** Every attempt to satisfy the demands creates another opportunity to invent a violation. The practical defence is to maintain independent, reasonable standards of behaviour rather than continually adapting to arbitrary rules. Refusing to participate in an impossible system of compliance is not, in itself, misconduct.

It's often combined with "Talking" to imply that your innocuous actions mean something significant that is not in compiance.
