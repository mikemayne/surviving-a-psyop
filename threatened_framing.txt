# Threatened Criminal Framing

**Effects:** Increase fear, Increase distress, Undermine confidence.

**Possible objectives:** Increase the effectiveness of other techniques, Induce compliance.

## The objective

The aim is to intimidate and control an individual by threatening to manufacture evidence, engineer incriminating circumstances or falsely accuse them of a serious criminal offence, particularly a sexual offence.

# How the technique works

The technique involves threatening to arrange circumstances in which an innocent individual could be accused of committing a serious crime. This might involve threats to fabricate evidence, make false witness statements, manipulate an innocent interaction to make it appear incriminating, or deliberately engineer a situation that could be misrepresented as criminal conduct.

Sexual offences are particularly potent subjects for this form of intimidation because the mere accusation can carry serious social, professional and personal consequences, regardless of whether criminal proceedings ultimately follow.

The threat need not be accompanied by an actual attempt to frame the individual. The possibility alone may be sufficient to create fear and influence their behaviour.

## Psychological mechanisms

- **Coercive intimidation:** Exploiting fear of criminal prosecution, imprisonment or reputational destruction to control behaviour.
- **Anticipatory anxiety:** Encouraging the individual to imagine catastrophic consequences that have not occurred, potentially making the threat psychologically powerful without requiring further action.
- **Reputational blackmail:** Exploiting the individual's fear of public humiliation, family rejection or social exclusion.
- **Hypervigilance:** Encouraging constant scrutiny of ordinary interactions and surroundings for anything that might later be misrepresented.
- **Learned helplessness:** Repeated threats may encourage the belief that behaving lawfully offers no protection because evidence or accusations could simply be manufactured.
- **Impossible compliance:** Presenting obedience as the only protection against a fabricated accusation, while retaining the ability to make the accusation regardless of compliance.

## Intended psychological effect

The central objective is to make the individual feel that their innocence offers no protection.

Unlike conventional intimidation, which threatens punishment for something the individual might do, threatened criminal framing suggests that punishment can be arranged irrespective of their behaviour.

This can be particularly destabilising because the individual cannot guarantee their safety simply by following the law or behaving reasonably.

The threat may also discourage the individual from reporting mistreatment, seeking assistance or challenging the person making the threat, particularly if they fear retaliation through a fabricated accusation.

## Response

**Don't engage at all. Record if convenient, then get on with your day.**

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, get to safety and call **999**. Seeking emergency help is not engaging with a provocation.

Read [the Golden Rule chapter](https://mikemayne.github.io/surviving-a-psyop/#read/never_engage).

**If you don't respond, they have nothing to work with.**

**If you don't react, you don't give them anything to work with.**
