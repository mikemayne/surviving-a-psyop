Up or Down - how to ignore repeated statements

The most important thing is to not engage with a statement from an abuser at all.  This can be very difficult because human nature makes engagement and conversation natural.  The easiest way I found to satiate the urge to engage when presented with a continuous stream of statements was to simply classify them as Up or Down, depending on what the intended effect on my emotional state appeared to be.  This has the benefit of allowing you to engage with the statement in an entirely dismissive way.  It's also much easier to group statements into two broad categories than it is to consider them carefully.

UP — Flattery, inflation or relief. Remarks that could increase pride, excitement, feelings of specialness or relief from criticism.  Appeals to your Ego.

Examples: "He's a genius." "He's going to be a millionaire." "He's finally worked everything out."

DOWN — Shame, discouragement or negging. Remarks that could increase insecurity, embarrassment, anger or the desire to defend yourself.

Examples: "Nobody will use that." "He's disgusting." "Everybody thinks he's an idiot."
