# Drugs and Alcohol

if you drink alcohol, be very careful not to become dependent on it.  Consider temporarily stopping altogether until the situation is resolved.  Be mindful that it can make your decisions more impulsive.  Be aware that the psychological pressure you are under increases the chances of you becoming dependent.  If you drink heavily, get medical advice and stop slowly.  Threats of prosecution can be used to intimidate someone who uses drugs, even if they are not dealing them.  If you use drugs frequently, and could be dependent, seek medical advice before stopping.  Drugs can also affect judgement and impulse control, although their effects vary.  Information about drug use can be used to shame someone or damage their relationships.  You might even be encouraged to take drugs by someone who then uses them to turn people against you.  If someone is actively encouraging you to use drugs or drink more, ask yourself why they are doing so.  In either case, if you are here my advice is you talk to a health professional about your situation.

## Response

**Don't engage at all. Record if convenient, then get on with your day.**

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, get to safety and call **999**. Seeking emergency help is not engaging with a provocation.

Read [the Golden Rule chapter](https://mikemayne.github.io/surviving-a-psyop/#read/never_engage).

**If you don't respond, they have nothing to work with.**

**If you don't react, you don't give them anything to work with.**
