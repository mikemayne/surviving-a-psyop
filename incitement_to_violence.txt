Incitement to Violence

This technique involves repeated minor crimes, provocative actions deliberately performed within view of CCTV cameras, or subtle insults and antagonistic remarks from strangers in public. These provocations may be combined with intentional sleep deprivation to impair judgement, reduce emotional regulation and increase impulsivity.  If you are being gaslit by friends it might be preceded by attempts to make you feel like you are hard, encouragement of toxic masculinity, even direct instructions to protect your family from the abusers when asked for advice what to do.

The psychological mechanisms include cumulative frustration, threat anticipation, emotional conditioning and reduced impulse control. No sophisticated technology is required; the technique relies entirely on psychological pressure and ordinary human behaviour.  It might include subtle negging from strangers in public or less subtle negging from a neighboring property. 

The objective would be to provoke the individual into committing an act of violence that is virtually certain to trigger a police response, while keeping the preceding provocations minor, ambiguous or difficult to investigate with the evidence left behind and the resources available to police.

The best course of action is to preserve evidence you already have, remain calm, and report relevant incidents to the police promptly and politely. Avoid arguing with people over insults or accusations, confronting them, chasing them or retaliating. **Not engaging does not mean failing to protect yourself from immediate physical harm.** If you are physically threatened, prioritise reaching safety and getting help; call 999 in an emergency. Report what you observed, keep your original CCTV footage, and separate facts from interpretations of motive.

## Response

**Don't engage at all. Record if convenient, then get on with your day.**

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, get to safety and call **999**. Seeking emergency help is not engaging with a provocation.

Read [the Golden Rule chapter](https://mikemayne.github.io/surviving-a-psyop/#read/never_engage).

**If you don't respond, they have nothing to work with.**

**If you don't react, you don't give them anything to work with.**
