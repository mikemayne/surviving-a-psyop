# Peripheral Disapproval

**Description**

People making negative facial expressions when you aren't looking at them directly, but they are still in your peripheral vision. They switch to a normal expression when you move and it looks likely you'll be looking directly at them.

**Timing Errors**

Sometimes people mistime the switch, and you see their expression change. This is a stronger signal than simply noticing a negative expression in your peripheral vision.

Deliberately ignore people's facial expressions in your peripheral vision. They are difficult to identify accurately, and previous experiences may have made you more likely to assume bad intentions where there are none.

Notice timing errors if they happen naturally, but don't look out for them. Don't monitor people or try to catch them changing expressions.

**Psychological Effect**

Can create a sense of social disapproval and encourage hypervigilance. Other psychological pressure techniques may already have left you suspicious of people's intentions, making you more likely to interpret innocent expressions as hostile.

**Response**

Don't engage at all. Record if convenient, then get on with your day.
