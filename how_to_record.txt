# How to Record

1. Send yourself emails detailing things you heard people say, as close to possible to their original words, including if possible the time and date, and where they were talking from.  Include how the comments made you feel at the time.  You might want to create a new specific email address for this purpose, I recommend gmail.  The reason you are sending them to yourself and not somebody else is you don't want to let someone else control the content of one of your emails to a third party.  Remember that recording accusations of crimes is not the same as admitting a crime.  If anything the fact you are willing to report the allegation makes you look less guilty.

2. Email yourself any CCTV evidence or other evidence with an brief summary of what you believe to have happened.  Stick to observable facts.  Record how the events made you feel.

3. Connect ChatGPT to your email account.  It can help search through your email for details, dates and give advice on correspondance.  Set ChatGPT to Candid.

4. As far as possible, use the same email address for all your correspondance with relevant authorities.  Keep all emails.  ChatGPT can read emails, give advice as to your rights and relevant laws.  It can help keep you grounded and suggest polite replies.
