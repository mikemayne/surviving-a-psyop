The only time to immediately engage with an abuser is if you feel you are in danger, and the only response to this is to call 999.

Otherwise the best strategy is Record, Ignore, Report.  

## Record

Don't let recording their statements ruin your life.  Record their words if convenient, ignore the bait completely and get on with your day.  If you have a spare five minutes, you can collect together everything you remember them saying for the day and email it to yourself.  Why email it to yourself?  Why not someone else? Because then they have control over what you email someone.  Don't let them control your actions.  

Email yourself.  Wait until you are calm, and feel rational.  Perhaps the next day, or a week later.  Only then decide what to do with the information.  The police are very unlikely to be able to do anything about a single statement with no witnesses, so there is no downside to waiting a day or two to report it through the non-emergency channel.  Then consider it carefully, report only what you heard someone say or saw someone do, don't try to assert what their intentions were, or claim that every bad thing that happens is connected.  Stick to the facts, in a coordinated campaign the facts will begin to speak for themselves.

## Ignore

The most hurtful thing you can do to an abuser is dismiss their statements out of hand.  

## Report

Early reporting is key to preventing abuse escalating.  Keep an open mind about mental health.  Make a summary of what has been happening, and how it has been making you feel and email it to your GP or mental health professional.  This is never a bad idea, and should be your first port of call.  If you have evidence of a crime, report it to the police.  Your statement alone can be used as evidence.  You might not have enough evidence for the police to allocate the resources to help you.  Accept this politely.  It's still your best course of action to report early, and you can always add additional information if the abuse continues.  Reporting early means it is more likely that CCTV, or other evidence still exists.
