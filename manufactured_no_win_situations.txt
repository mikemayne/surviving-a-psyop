# Manufactured No-Win Situations

**Effects:** Increase frustration, Increase uncertainty, Undermine confidence.

**Possible objectives:** Create compliance pressure, Discourage independent decisions, Keep an individual defending themselves.

## The principle

Construct a choice, rule or demand so that every available response can be treated as wrong.

The person may comply, refuse, explain or disengage, yet the standard changes to make each response another supposed failure.

## How it works

Imagine someone demands an apology. If the person apologises, they are told the apology proves guilt. If they refuse, they are accused of lacking remorse. If they ask what they did, they are told they should already know.

The apparent choices are different, but the judgement has been decided in advance.

The same principle can operate through contradictory instructions, retrospective rules, arbitrary tests and impossible standards.

**The mechanism is a closed system of judgement, not merely a difficult choice.**

An analyser should identify the permitted responses, the consequence assigned to each, and whether any reasonable response could satisfy the stated demand. A genuinely hard decision is not necessarily a manufactured trap.

This meta-pattern connects Impossible Compliance, Impossible Standards, Logic Traps, False Endings and Loaded Questions.

## Response

**Don't engage at all. Record if convenient, then get on with your day.**
