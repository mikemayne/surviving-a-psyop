# Humiliation Rituals

**Effects:** Increase shame, Increase fear, Undermine confidence.

**Possible objectives:** Increase the effectiveness of other techniques, Induce compliance.

## The technique

A humiliation ritual is a demand that someone perform, endure or acknowledge something degrading as a supposed condition of acceptance, forgiveness, discipline or continued assistance. It might involve an unnecessary apology, a degrading posture, the public exposure of private bodily matters, or a forced confession to a trivial or invented offence. The demand is often presented as a test of obedience rather than a response to any genuine problem.

## How it works

Humiliation attacks a person's sense of dignity and belonging. People may comply simply to end embarrassment or avoid a greater punishment. Having complied once, they may feel ashamed and reluctant to tell anyone. The person imposing the ritual can then reinterpret compliance as consent, guilt, or evidence that the target accepts a subordinate role. Repetition can normalise behaviour that would once have seemed obviously unacceptable.

## Recognising the pattern

An appropriate request may be uncomfortable without being abusive; for example, medical care sometimes involves necessary intimate examinations, and genuine wrongdoing may call for an apology. A humiliation ritual adds degradation with little or no legitimate purpose. Questions to ask include whether the act is necessary, whether privacy and consent are respected, whether a less degrading alternative exists, and whether refusal leads to retaliation.

## Response

**Don't engage at all. Record if convenient, then get on with your day.**

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, get to safety and call **999**. Seeking emergency help is not engaging with a provocation.

Read [the Golden Rule chapter](https://mikemayne.github.io/surviving-a-psyop/#read/never_engage).

**If you don't respond, they have nothing to work with.**

**If you don't react, you don't give them anything to work with.**
