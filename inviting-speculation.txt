# Inviting Speculation

"You have no idea who we are."

"You have no idea what's coming."

"You wouldn't believe what we're capable of."

These statements provide almost no useful information. Instead, they invite you to speculate about the identity, resources, capabilities and intentions of whoever is speaking.

This can be an effective psychological trick. Your imagination may produce explanations far more frightening than anything explicitly stated. Every new possibility creates another question, potentially drawing you into an endless cycle of speculation.

**Don't fill in the blanks for them.** An extraordinary claim without supporting evidence tells you very little. Someone implying that they possess enormous power does not establish that they do.

You don't need to discover who someone is, what they might be capable of or what they supposedly have planned. If a statement amounts to a credible threat, record and report it through the appropriate channels. Otherwise, decline the invitation to speculate.

**Don't engage.**
