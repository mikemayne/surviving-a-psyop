# Negging — Psychological Manipulation

**Effects:** Increase emotional reactivity, Undermine confidence, Increase shame.

**Possible objectives:** Increase the effectiveness of other techniques, Provoke a reaction.

## The objective

The aim is to undermine an individual's confidence, provoke an emotional reaction, or encourage them to seek approval by exposing them to subtle insults, dismissive comments, backhanded compliments, or apparently innocent remarks.

## How it works

Negging exploits sensitivity to social rejection, embarrassment, insecurity, and the human tendency to interpret ambiguous social interactions. Comments may be sufficiently subtle or indirect that the recipient is uncertain whether an insult was intended. This ambiguity encourages rumination and self-doubt while providing the speaker with plausible deniability. Repeated exposure can increase self-consciousness, vigilance, and sensitivity to perceived criticism, particularly during periods of stress or sleep deprivation.

## The technique

No technology or specialist knowledge is required. Negging may be carried out by complete strangers, acquaintances, or people the individual considers friends. An apparently casual remark, joke, or dismissive gesture can be enough to draw attention to a perceived personal weakness. The technique is most effective when the recipient responds emotionally, becomes preoccupied with interpreting the interaction, or changes their behaviour to obtain approval.

## Response

**Don't engage at all. Record if convenient, then get on with your day.**

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, get to safety and call **999**. Seeking emergency help is not engaging with a provocation.

Read [the Golden Rule chapter](https://mikemayne.github.io/surviving-a-psyop/#read/never_engage).

**If you don't respond, they have nothing to work with.**

**If you don't react, you don't give them anything to work with.**
