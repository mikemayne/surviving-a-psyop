# Credibility Sabotage Through Induced Exaggeration

**Effects:** Anxiety, escalating worry.

**Possible objectives:** Magnify threats.

### THE TECHNIQUE

This technique involves encouraging an individual to make increasingly exaggerated, speculative or implausible claims, potentially undermining the credibility of their legitimate complaints.

A person might introduce extraordinary explanations for otherwise ordinary events, encourage increasingly elaborate interpretations, or repeatedly suggest that unrelated incidents form part of a single coordinated scheme.

The objective, when used deliberately, is to make the individual's account appear unreliable, irrational or delusional, potentially causing other people to dismiss even those parts that are accurate and independently verifiable.

### WHY IT WORKS

Human beings tend to assess the credibility of an account as a whole rather than independently evaluating every individual claim.

If someone presents several demonstrably accurate observations alongside extraordinary or unsupported conclusions, a listener may reject the entire account rather than distinguishing between its stronger and weaker elements.

This exploits a form of **credibility contamination**, in which the perceived implausibility of one claim damages confidence in unrelated claims.

The technique can be especially damaging when an individual is already distressed, sleep-deprived or struggling to understand confusing experiences. They may become more receptive to suggested explanations, particularly when those explanations appear to make sense of otherwise disconnected events.

### RECOGNISING THE PATTERN

Warning signs include someone repeatedly encouraging you to speculate beyond what you personally observed, suggesting increasingly elaborate explanations without evidence, or pressuring you to express uncertain conclusions as established facts.

Another warning sign is someone attempting to put words into your mouth, then treating those words as your own independently formed beliefs.

The distinguishing feature is pressure to move from **“This is what I experienced”** to **“This is definitely what happened and why.”**

### Further considerations
Keep observations, interpretations and hypotheses clearly separated.

Describe precisely what you saw, heard or experienced without embellishment. Where there is uncertainty, acknowledge it rather than attempting to construct a complete explanation.

Avoid adopting somebody else's interpretation simply because they express it confidently or because it appears to explain several experiences simultaneously.

When reporting concerns, provide the strongest independently verifiable facts first. An account does not need to explain everything to justify reasonable enquiries into specific incidents.

**The central principle is that you do not need an extraordinary explanation for your experiences to deserve to have concrete concerns taken seriously.**

## Response

**Don't engage at all. Record if convenient, then get on with your day.**

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, get to safety and call **999**. Seeking emergency help is not engaging with a provocation.

Read [the Golden Rule chapter](https://mikemayne.github.io/surviving-a-psyop/#read/never_engage).

**If you don't respond, they have nothing to work with.**

**If you don't react, you don't give them anything to work with.**
