# The Fabricated Confession

**Effects:** Increase confusion, Increase shame, Increase distress.

**Possible objectives:** Increase the effectiveness of other techniques, Discredit the individual.

**Example:** "He just said [insert admission of a crime]."

## The pattern

This is a claim that somebody has just confessed to a crime, made in the third person as though the speaker is reporting a fact. In the fabricated version of the pattern, the supposed confession never happened.

The wording matters. "He just said" suggests an immediate eyewitness account. It can give a serious accusation the appearance of an observation without providing the alleged speaker's actual words or any independent confirmation.

A statement *about* somebody is not a statement *made by* them. Hearing a person claim that someone confessed does not establish that a confession took place.

## How It Could Be Used

**False attribution.** A speaker may attribute words to somebody who never said them, making an accusation sound like a quotation or firsthand report.

**Pressure to defend.** A serious accusation can create a powerful urge to interrupt, deny it, explain what was actually said or demand that the speaker withdraw it. If the statement is provocative, that reaction can turn it into an unwanted exchange.

**Doubt about memory.** Repeatedly insisting that someone said something they do not remember saying can make them question their recollection, especially when stressed or tired. This can resemble gaslighting when it forms part of a persistent effort to undermine confidence in memory or perception. A single unsupported assertion is more precisely called false attribution; it does not, by itself, establish gaslighting.

**Reputational harm.** A listener might repeat a supposed confession as though it were genuine. The allegation can then travel further than the original words, regardless of whether anyone checks what was actually said.

## Response

**Don't engage at all. Record if convenient, then get on with your day.**

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, get to safety and call **999**. Seeking emergency help is not engaging with a provocation.

Read [the Golden Rule chapter](https://mikemayne.github.io/surviving-a-psyop/#read/never_engage).

**If you don't respond, they have nothing to work with.**

**If you don't react, you don't give them anything to work with.**
