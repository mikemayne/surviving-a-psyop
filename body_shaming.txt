### Body Shaming and Humiliation in Private Spaces

**Objective**

To undermine an individual's dignity, confidence and sense of bodily autonomy by subjecting them to degrading comments about their physical appearance or bodily functions while they are showering, using the toilet or otherwise engaged in intimate personal activities. The objective is to exploit moments of vulnerability to induce shame, embarrassment and self-consciousness, potentially making even ordinary private activities a source of distress.

**Psychological Techniques**

This combines body shaming, sexualised humiliation, degradation, invasion of privacy and shame conditioning. It exploits the individual's expectation of privacy during intimate activities, together with common sensitivities about physical appearance, sexuality and bodily functions.

**Mechanism**

The individual is subjected to mocking, insulting or degrading comments about their body, genitals, physical characteristics or ordinary bodily functions, particularly during activities in which they would reasonably expect privacy.

The criticism may focus on entirely normal physical features or behaviours, deliberately portraying them as disgusting, abnormal or embarrassing.

Repeating such comments during private activities can create anticipatory anxiety, causing the individual to become excessively self-conscious or uncomfortable in situations that should be entirely routine.

The technique may also exploit a perceived invasion of privacy. Comments suggesting that intimate activities are being observed can be distressing even without any established observation, because they encourage the individual to question whether their privacy is secure.

**Countermeasure**

Recognise that ordinary bodily functions and physical characteristics are not legitimate grounds for humiliation. Avoid evaluating your body according to deliberately degrading remarks or attempting to satisfy arbitrary standards imposed by someone behaving abusively.

Maintain ordinary personal routines, distinguish actual privacy concerns from threatening insinuations, and address any concrete intrusion through appropriate channels.

**The human body is not shameful, and nobody forfeits their right to dignity and privacy by engaging in ordinary personal activities.**
