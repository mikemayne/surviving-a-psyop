# Presence Threat

**Effects:** Fear, Hypervigilance, Anticipatory anxiety.

**Possible objectives:** Intimidation, Disrupt normal behaviour, Increase the effectiveness of other techniques.

## The technique

The technique is to make someone aware of a threatening person's presence without making any explicit threat.

They let you know **they're there, and when they're there**. They don't need to approach you, speak to you or do anything overtly threatening.

The implied message is: *We're here. We can reach you whenever we want.*

If deliberately used for intimidation, the effect can persist after the person has gone. The target may begin anticipating their presence, checking their surroundings and changing their behaviour.

**The threat comes from the meaning attached to the presence, rather than anything the person actually does.**

## Response

**Don't engage at all. Record if convenient, then get on with your day.**

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, get to safety and call **999**. Seeking emergency help is not engaging with a provocation.

Read [the Golden Rule chapter](https://mikemayne.github.io/surviving-a-psyop/#read/never_engage).

**If you don't respond, they have nothing to work with.**

**If you don't react, you don't give them anything to work with.**
