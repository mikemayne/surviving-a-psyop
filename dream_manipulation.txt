# Dream Manipulation

**Effects:** Increase confusion, Increase distress, Undermine confidence.

**Possible objectives:** Increase the effectiveness of other techniques, Provoke a reaction.

## The effect

Sounds heard during sleep can sometimes become incorporated into dreams. For lucid dreamers, who know they are dreaming while the dream is happening, the experience can feel particularly vivid and memorable.

A combination of auditory suggestion, cold reading and suggestibility after waking could make it seem as though someone knows about, or has influenced, a dream. None of these effects requires mind-reading technology.

## How it could work

- **Dream incorporation:** A sound or spoken phrase heard while sleeping may occasionally influence what appears in a dream. This is not a reliable way to direct the content of someone's dreams.
- **Cold reading:** After the person wakes, broad guesses, ambiguous statements and reactions to their answers can make someone appear to know details they were never told.
- **Post-waking suggestibility:** Dream memories are often incomplete. Leading questions or suggestions can influence how a person remembers or interprets a dream, particularly when they have only just woken up.
- **Selective matches:** A few apparent successes may stand out, while guesses that were wrong or too vague to assess are overlooked.
- **Claiming credit:** Someone might claim to have caused a dream after hearing a description of it, without having influenced it at all.

These effects could create an impression of sophisticated dream manipulation even when the apparent knowledge came from ordinary guessing, memory reconstruction or coincidence. It is also possible to have a vivid, unusual dream without anyone else being involved.

## Research gap

Sound incorporation into dreams is documented, but reliable control of specific dream content or accurate inference of an unreported dream from waking behaviour has not been demonstrated. Sleep-laboratory research with informed consent can examine limited incorporation effects.

## Response

**Don't engage at all. Record if convenient, then get on with your day.**

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, get to safety and call **999**. Seeking emergency help is not engaging with a provocation.

Read [the Golden Rule chapter](https://mikemayne.github.io/surviving-a-psyop/#read/never_engage).

**If you don't respond, they have nothing to work with.**

**If you don't react, you don't give them anything to work with.**
