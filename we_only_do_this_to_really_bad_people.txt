# We Only Do This to Really Bad People

A person attempting psychological manipulation might claim that their tactics are reserved for particularly bad people:

- "We only do this to the REALLY bad ones."
- "We wouldn't be doing this if you were innocent."
- "People like you deserve everything they get."

### How it works

The technique attempts to turn the alleged punishment into evidence of guilt.

Rather than presenting evidence of wrongdoing, the speaker implies that the decision to mistreat someone must already have been justified.

It exploits several psychological mechanisms:

- **Implied guilt:** If only bad people receive this treatment, receiving it supposedly means you are bad.
- **Appeal to authority:** The speaker implies that someone has already investigated and judged you, without providing evidence.
- **Moral justification:** Cruelty is presented as a necessary or deserved response to wrongdoing.
- **Shame and self-doubt:** The target may begin questioning whether they have somehow done something terrible without realising it.

The reasoning is circular: *We only punish guilty people. We're punishing you. Therefore you must be guilty.*

This framing may also discourage someone from seeking help. They may worry that police, doctors or other authorities will automatically assume they deserve the treatment they describe.

### How to respond

Recognise the logical flaw. Someone claiming that you deserve mistreatment does not establish that you have done anything wrong.

You don't need to defend yourself against unspecified accusations or convince the speaker of your innocence.

Disengage, avoid arguing, and carry on with your ordinary activities. If there is a genuine allegation, it should be addressed through an appropriate process where specific claims can be examined fairly.
