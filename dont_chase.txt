# Don't Chase

Don't chase them.  Trying to catch them is counter-productive.  Especially avoid doing anything unethical or illegal in your attempts to catch them, this will be used against you. 

You cannot assume who anyone is on the basis of an overheard remark or an ambiguous event. Trying to work out who is involved can become another distraction from ordinary life.  

They want you to engage with them.  Ignoring them means they either have to go away, or behave increasingly recklessly to try to get your attention.  In this case they are generating more evidence.  Ignoring them can be difficult, so use the advice on the first page to avoid engaging with manipulative tactics.  

They will misrepresent who they are at every step of the way, so if they tell you something write it down if you want to, but don't pay any attention to it.  A claim about someone's identity or capabilities is not evidence that the claim is true.

## Response

**Don't engage at all. Record if convenient, then get on with your day.**

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, get to safety and call **999**. Seeking emergency help is not engaging with a provocation.

Read [the Golden Rule chapter](https://mikemayne.github.io/surviving-a-psyop/#read/never_engage).

**If you don't respond, they have nothing to work with.**

**If you don't react, you don't give them anything to work with.**
